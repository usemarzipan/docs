The subscription detail screen shows one customer's individual instance of a package, and gives you every action you need to manage it.

To open a subscription:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Click on **Subscriptions** in the main navigation menu
3. Click on any subscription in the list

## The subscription detail screen

A subscription has three tabs: **Overview**, **Shipments** and **Activity**.

The **Overview** shows:

- **Highlights** - lifetime value, how long it has been active, the next shipment, the number of shipments sent, the frequency and the value of any add-on items
- **Activity** - the latest actions on the subscription, where you can also add a note
- **Related orders** - orders generated by the subscription
- **Customer** - the subscriber, linking to their customer record
- **Details** - status, package, price, frequency, start date, **Next billing** and **Next shipping**
- **Shipping** - how the subscription is delivered, with **Edit** to change it
- **Payment method** - the card renewals are charged to

On a package that ships on its own dates, **Next shipping** is the date of the next dated shipment, which can differ from **Next billing**. On a [package that sends nothing](/subscriptions/packages#a-package-that-sends-nothing) there is no **Next shipping**, no **Shipping** box and no **Shipments** tab, and the highlights show the **Next payment** instead.

### Shipping

The **Shipping** box shows the **Preferred option** (delivery or a collection point), the delivery address, phone and delivery instructions, and the **Delivery charge**: what each shipment is charged for delivery. It reads **Included in the package**, **None (collected)**, an amount per shipment with its delivery method, or **Free**. The charge is the one the subscriber's postcode gets, so delivery limited to certain postcodes is quoted correctly.

### Payment method

A new subscription renews on the card that paid for it, even where the customer has another card saved as their default. Checkout tells the customer their card is kept for renewals, in wording you can change under **Settings > Branding & Web Components > Checkout** (**Saved card message**).

## The Shipments tab

The **Shipments** tab shows what the subscription sends.

- **Next shipment** - what goes in the next shipment, with its price, delivery, tax and total. While a shipment is being processed it reads **Current shipment**
- **Upcoming shipments** - later shipments already scheduled, with their dates
- **Past shipments** - everything already sent, newest first, with its date, its order, what went and the order's status. Click a row to open the order

A renewal that only took a payment and sent nothing isn't listed under past shipments. An ended subscription keeps its **Shipments** tab for its past shipments, and the **Shipments** count in the highlights matches the list.

What the next shipment shows depends on the package:

- **Pick & Mix** - the customer's picks, with **Update pick and mix items** to change them
- **Fixed** - the package and, under it, the product and how many are sent. See [Product subscriptions](/subscriptions/product-subscriptions)
- **Shipments on their own dates** - the next dated shipment: its items, the date it ships, its delivery and its total. It is shown apart from the subscriber's payments, which carry on at their own frequency. If none is dated yet, it says so

### Additional items

Click **Add additional items** (or **Manage additional items**) to add products to the next shipment. They are charged and sent with it. Tick **Add to every shipment** to keep them on every shipment rather than the next one only, and **Notify customer of changes** to email the customer.

On a package that ships on its own dates, additional items go in the next dated shipment and are charged on its order with its delivery, on top of the subscriber's payments.

## Actions on a subscription

The **Actions** menu offers:

- **Manual Renewal** - process the next renewal now. Available when the next billing date is within 30 days either way and there has been no order in the last 24 hours. On a variable package it needs an upcoming shipment
- **Send renewal email** - email the customer a payment link, valid for 7 days
- **Transfer subscription** - move the subscription to another customer
- **Change package** - move the subscription onto another package
- **Skip next shipment** - miss the next shipment, with a comment
- **Cancel subscription** - cancel it, optionally emailing the customer
- **Reactivate subscription** - restart a cancelled, expired or suspended subscription

You can also change the frequency, the next billing date and the preferred shipping date with **Edit** beside each in the **Details** box.

### Renewals

**Manual Renewal** queues the renewal straight away rather than waiting for the cycle. It can take a few minutes, and you may need to refresh to see the result.

Automatic renewal reminders are configured on the package. See [Renewal options](/subscriptions/packages#renewal-options).

### Changing a subscription

You can **transfer** a subscription to another customer, **change the package** the customer is on, or **change the frequency** they are billed at.

**Change package** moves the subscription onto any other package, on the same terms as a customer [switching from their account](/subscriptions/packages#switching-package): from the next payment, at the new package's price, with nothing charged on the day. It isn't available on a [gift subscription](/subscriptions/gifts), whose package the giver chose and is paying for.

Changing package or frequency keeps a subscriber billed on the 30th on the 30th, rather than moving them to the month end.

Customers can also switch package themselves, onto the packages you list on theirs. The activity trail names the package they moved from and links the one they moved to, and earlier entries keep the package they were about rather than taking the new name.

### Skipping a shipment

**Skip next shipment** misses one shipment without cancelling the subscription, and the payment moves on with it. On a variable package the next payment falls on the date of the shipment after the skipped one; on other packages it moves a billing period on. The subscription's end date moves with it, so it doesn't expire early.

On a package that ships on its own dates, skipping holds back the next dated shipment only. The subscriber's payments carry on as normal.

Where a customer skips from their [account](/web-components/account) they are asked why, and the reason they gave shows against the skip in the activity trail.

### Cancelling and reactivating

**Cancel subscription** shows the subscription as cancelled, though it stays valid until the end of the current period. Leave **Send cancellation email to customer** ticked to tell them. A cancelled subscription can be restarted with **Reactivate subscription**.

You can cancel at any time, including within a package's [minimum term](/subscriptions/packages#renewal-options). Customers can't cancel from their account until the term ends, and are shown the date they can cancel from.

Where a customer cancels from their [account](/web-components/account), the account asks why before it confirms, and offers whatever the package can do about it: the next shipment skipped, a less frequent schedule, or [another package](/subscriptions/packages#switching-package). A cheaper package is offered to customers who say it costs too much, that it's too much, or that they're cutting down, and any listed package to those who don't enjoy it. Backing out of an offer returns them to the others rather than out of cancelling. "Would rather not say" is offered outright, so nobody has to invent a reason to get out.

The reason they gave is shown on the subscription as **Cancel reason** beside its cancelled date, and named in the activity trail.

## Failed renewals

If a renewal payment fails, the subscription shows a warning. Marzipan retries the payment up to four times, then cancels the subscription.

<Warning>
Once the customer has fixed their card, you may need to trigger a **Manual Renewal** to get them back on track.
</Warning>

### Waiting for a shipment

A variable package with scheduled dates bills on its shipments' dates. If a subscription's billing date arrives with no shipment scheduled, it can't renew, and the subscription says so.

Schedule a shipment for the package and every subscription waiting for one moves onto the package's next scheduled shipment and renews on its date. You don't need to do anything to each subscription.
