Subscriptions power your plans and recurring shipments in Marzipan.

There are two things to understand:

- A **package** (also called a plan) is the template a customer subscribes to.
- A **subscription** is one customer's individual instance of that package. The customer is its **subscriber**, and what it sends them are its **shipments**.

### Package types

Packages come in three **types**:

- **Variable** - you choose the products in each shipment, and shipments go out on the dates you set. A variable package can bill everyone together on each shipment's date, bill each subscriber on their own anniversary and send whichever shipment is current, or bill on anniversaries and send its dated shipments to everyone. See [Shipment dating](/subscriptions/packages#shipment-dating)
- **Pick & Mix** - the customer chooses their own items each cycle, within the limits you set
- **Fixed** - one product on repeat, which customers can subscribe to from the product's own page. See [Product subscriptions](/subscriptions/product-subscriptions)

### Pricing

A package has a single price, except where:

- A **pick & mix** package is priced per item, so each subscriber pays for what they pick
- A **fixed** package is priced as a percentage off its product's price, which it follows, or at its own price per item. The subscriber pays it times how many they chose

### The Subscriber tag

Customers with an active subscription are automatically given a **Subscriber** tag. This tag is used for subscriber pricing and for building customer segments.

## Subscriptions

To view your subscriptions:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Click on **Subscriptions** in the main navigation menu
3. The list displays every customer subscription across all of your packages

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/subscriptions/subscriptions-list.png" alt="Subscriptions list" />
</Frame>

The list has the following columns:

- **Customer** - the subscriber
- **Package** - the package they subscribe to
- **Started** - when the subscription began
- **Renews** - the next renewal date
- **Type** - `fixed`, `variable` or `picknmix`
- **Market** - only shown on multi-market stores
- **Status** - the current subscription status

Click on any subscription in the list to view its [subscription details](/subscriptions/details).

### Filtering and searching

You can refine the list with:

- Filter by **item**
- Filter by **Status** - Active, Cancelled, Expired or Suspended
- Filter by **market**
- Filter by **start date**
- A **Cancelled/Expired date range**
- **Search**

### Exporting subscriptions

Click **Export** to download the subscriptions in the table as a CSV file. Along with each subscriber's details, package, price and status, it includes their **Next Billing Date** and **Next Shipping Date**, the **Next Shipment** they'll receive, their delivery details and **Delivery Charge**, any **Additional Items**, and when and why they cancelled.

The next shipment is the one their next renewal will actually send, so a six-monthly subscriber isn't shown a shipment that goes before their payment. On a package that ships on its own dates, it is the next dated shipment.

<Tip>
Apply filters _before_ clicking the export button to only export the subscriptions you need.
</Tip>

### Subscription Insights

For reporting on your plans, use **Subscription Insights**, where you can review performance and **export** the underlying data. See [Insights](/insights/overview) for more.
