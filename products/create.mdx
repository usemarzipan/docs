To create a product, go to **Products** in the main navigation menu, click **Add product**, and choose a type. To edit an existing product, click it in the list. Both open the same form.

<Note>
The product **type** (**Physical** or **Bundle**) is set at creation and can't be changed afterwards. See [Overview](/products/overview) for what each type is used for.
</Note>

<Steps titleSize="p">
  <Step title="Add the product details">
    - **Name** - the product name shown to customers
    - **Slug** - the URL-friendly version of the name, generated automatically and editable
    - **Type** - Physical or Bundle (only shown while creating, as it can't be changed)
    - **Summary** - a short rich-text introduction
    - **Description** - the full rich-text product description. Use the embed button on its toolbar to paste in video embed code, such as a Bunny Stream or YouTube embed snippet.
    - **SKU** - your stock-keeping reference
    - **Barcode** - accepts an EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, or ITF-14 code, validated live as you type
    - **Google category** - only shown when [Google Merchant Center](/integrations/google-merchant) is connected. Leave it blank to use the integration's default.
    - **Collections** - the [collections](/collections/overview) this product belongs to
    - **Availability** - who can buy the product: **Everyone** or **Subscribers only**
    - **Status** - the product's current status
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it's visible">
    For multi-market stores, **Market Visibility** controls which markets show the product.

    <Tip>
    If you don't select any markets, the product is visible everywhere.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add images and videos">
    <Frame>
    <img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/products/product-images.png" alt="Product images" />
    </Frame>

    - **Images** - add product images from your [media library](/media/overview) and drag to reorder them. The first image is used as the product's main image across your store, cart and checkout.
    - **Videos** - a gallery can also hold videos. Upload an MP4, MOV or WebM file (up to 2GB) to the [media library](/media/overview#video), then add it to the gallery like an image.

    A new video shows **Processing video** while it's prepared. It might take up to 15 minutes, and it goes live on its own, so you can leave the page. Give each video a title: it's what screen readers announce for the player.

    <Note>
    A video is never the main image. The main image is always the first still in the gallery, and a video is left out of places that can only show a still, such as Google Merchant Center. On the storefront, a video plays only when the shopper chooses it. Set the player's controls and colour on the **Video** tab under **Settings > Branding & Web Components**.
    </Note>

    For multi-market stores, the gallery has an **All markets** tab plus one tab per market, and each image carries a **market scope**. Leave it unset and the image shows everywhere; set it and the image only shows in the markets you choose. Use this when the same SKU needs different imagery per market, usually because the physical label differs. Front and back label shots go in the gallery like any other image.

    <Note>
    The main image is simply the first image available in the market being viewed, so a market with its own imagery leads with it. If scoping would leave a product with no image at all, the full gallery is shown instead.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the product with attributes">
    Assign [attribute](/attributes/overview) values, such as Size, Colour, or Material, to describe the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the SEO details">
    The **SEO & Social** section controls how the product appears in search results and when its page is shared.

    - **Meta title** - the title shown in search results
    - **Meta description** - the description shown in search results
    - **Share image** - the Open Graph image used when the product is shared
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rest of the product up">
    The remaining sections of the form are covered on their own pages:

    - [Pricing](/products/pricing)
    - [Inventory](/products/inventory)
    - [Bundles and options](/products/bundles-and-options)
    - [Reviews and awards](/products/reviews-and-awards)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Offering it as a subscription

Once a product is saved, its **Subscription** section lists the packages that send it on repeat, each with its price or saving, its number of subscribers and how many are due in the next 30 days. Click a row to open the package, or click **Create subscription** to start a new package for this product. See [Product subscriptions](/subscriptions/product-subscriptions).

## Viewing it on your store

Once a product is saved, the **View product** button beside the page title opens it on your storefront in a new tab.

<Tip>
Already selling something similar? Use **Duplicate product** from the **Actions** menu to create a copy as a starting point.
</Tip>
