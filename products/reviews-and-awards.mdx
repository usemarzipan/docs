Awards and reviews are set on the product form. Open a product from **Products** in the main navigation menu and scroll to the relevant section.

## Awards

Add awards to a product, each with:

- **Award** — the award itself
- **Event** — the competition or event it was given at
- **Class** — the class the product was entered in
- **Year** — the year it was awarded

## Reviews

Add reviews to a product, each with:

- **Author** — who wrote the review
- **Review** — the review text
- **Type** — the kind of review
- **Date** — when it was written

<Note>
Each award and review can be set to **published** or **private**. Private entries are kept on the product but aren't shown to customers.
</Note>
