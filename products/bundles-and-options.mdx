These sections of the product form let you build up what a customer can buy, and what you show them alongside it. Open a product from **Products** in the main navigation menu to find them.

## Bundle items

For a **bundle** product, choose the products included in the bundle and the quantity of each.

<Note>
Bundle items only appear on products created with the **Bundle** type. The type can't be changed after a product is created.
</Note>

## Options

Use **Add option** to give a product options the customer chooses or fills in before adding it to their cart. Each option has a **Name** and a **Type**:

- **Dropdown** - a list of choices, for example Small, Medium and Large. Type each value into **Available Options** and press Enter or a comma.
- **Short text** - a text box for up to 100 characters on a single line, for example an engraving or a name on a label
- **Long text** - a text box for up to 500 characters, over several lines if they like, for example a gift message

Turn on **Required** so the customer must pick a choice, or type something, before the product can go in their cart.

On the storefront, a text option shows as a text box with a character count, and the add to cart button waits until any required text is filled in. Product cards in a collection have nowhere to type, so a product with a required text option links through to its product page rather than adding straight to the cart.

<Note>
The same product added twice with different choices or text goes in the cart as two lines, so a second engraving never overwrites the first. What the customer typed keeps its line breaks and capitalisation on the order and in their emails.
</Note>

## Upsell and related products

- **Upsell products** - alternatives a customer can choose instead of this product, for example a case of wine or a bottle and gift box. Drag them into order, and give each a short storefront label for the list of options; leave it blank to use the product name.
- **Related products** - products, events, subscription packages or vouchers shown alongside this one

## Tier exclusivity

If you use reward tiers, **Tier Exclusivity** in the Inventory section restricts a product to specific tiers, so only customers in those tiers can buy it.
