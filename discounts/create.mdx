To create a discount:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Navigate to **Discounts** in the main menu
3. Click the **Add discount** button in the top-right corner

Clicking a discount in the list opens the same form for editing.

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/discounts/create-discount.png" alt="Create discount form" />
</Frame>

The form runs in sections, in the order you'd answer them: the discount itself, what it applies to, who can use it, what the cart needs, how often it can be used, and when. Each condition is a toggle: turn one on and its settings appear beneath it.

## The discount

- **Discount code** - the code customers enter at checkout. Use **Generate code** to create one automatically.
- **Description** - shown to customers at checkout, and in subscription emails when the discount is a subscription benefit.
- **Apply automatically** - applied automatically when a cart qualifies, without the customer needing to enter a code.
- **Type** - how the discount reduces the price (see below).
- **Amount** - the amount or percentage off, for **Fixed amount** and **Percent** discounts.

### Type

- **Fixed amount** - takes a set amount off.
- **Percent** - takes a percentage off.
- **Free shipping** - removes shipping costs.
- **Free item** - adds a product to the order for free. Choose the **Free item** and set an **Item label prefix**, the text put before the product name, such as `FREE`.
- **Cheapest item free** - takes the cheapest qualifying item already in the cart off the total. One unit, not the whole line, so three bottles at £10 is £10 off.

<Note>
**Free item** adds a product you nominate to the cart. **Cheapest item free** discounts something the customer has already chosen. The cheapest item is picked from the same set your product, product-type and pricing conditions already define, so a discount limited to a collection only ever gives away an item from it.
</Note>

### Market

For multi-market stores, choose the **Market** the discount applies to. A market is **required for fixed amount** discounts; for all other types it's optional and the discount applies to all markets when left blank.

<Note>
A fixed amount is a sum of money, so it needs a market to tell it which currency to use. Percentage and free-shipping discounts work in any currency, so they don't.
</Note>

## What it applies to

- **Specific products only** - limit the discount to the **Products** you choose, which can include events and subscriptions.
- **Specific product types only** - limit it to **Physical**, **Bundle**, **Subscription** or **Event** items.
- **Don't discount sale prices** - leave items that are already on sale at their sale price.
- **Don't discount subscriber prices** - products with a subscriber price aren't discounted any further.

<Tip>
Use the two pricing exclusions when you run a broad promotion but don't want it compounding on items that are already reduced.
</Tip>

## Who can use it

- **Customer must have an active subscription** - only customers with an active subscription can use the discount. Under **Package must be one of**, pick the packages that qualify, or leave it empty for any package.
- **Customer must have one of these tags** - only customers with at least one of the **Tags** you enter can use it.

Both conditions are about the **customer**, not what's in the cart. To require a subscription in the cart, use **Cart must contain a subscription** under Requirements.

<Tip>
A package's **Benefits** card lists the discounts limited to its subscribers, and its **Add** button opens this form with the package already picked. Saving or cancelling takes you back to the package. See [Packages](/subscriptions/packages).
</Tip>

## Requirements

- **Minimum spend** - the amount the cart must reach, measured before any discounts or shipping fees.
- **Minimum items** - the total quantity the cart must hold, for offers framed by count rather than value. Leave blank for no requirement. Giveaway lines aren't counted, so a free item can't help satisfy the condition that produced it.
- **Cart must contain a subscription** - the discount only applies when someone subscribes. It still comes off everything it applies to, for example a free bottle, or 10% off the whole order.
- **Specific shipping methods only** - pick the **Shipping methods** the discount works with, from a list like the market visibility picker.

## Limits

- **Limit total uses** - cap how many times the discount can be used across every customer, under **Uses**.
- **Limit per customer** - cap how much each customer can use it, set as an **Allowance** (see below). This is based on the customer's email address, so it counts guest checkouts too.
- **Can be used with other discounts** - let this discount apply alongside others in the same cart. It's off by default.

### Per-customer allowance

The allowance reads as one line: a number, then **orders** or **items**, then **in total**, **every month** or **every year**. For example:

- **1 order in total** - the classic one use per customer
- **3 orders every month** - up to three orders a month get the discount
- **2 items every year** - the discount applies to up to two items a year, which can be spread over more than one order. Any more are charged in full, and the cheapest are discounted first.

An info box under the allowance says it back as a sentence, naming the items it covers. Counting in items uses whatever you chose under **What it applies to**, so pick products there if the allowance should only cover certain things, such as tour tickets.

<Note>
A subscriber's month or year runs from when their subscription started; anyone else's goes by the calendar. Cancelled, failed and draft orders, and orders refunded in full, give their use back.
</Note>

<Tip>
For a subscriber perk such as two free tour tickets a year, make a **Percent** discount of 100% on the tour, limit it to subscribers, and set the allowance to **2 items every year**. The units it gives away show as free in the cart, with the discount's description, just as **Cheapest item free** does.
</Tip>

## Dates

Set **Valid from** and **Valid until** dates to control when the discount is active. Leave **Valid until** blank if the discount doesn't expire.
