---
title: 'Groups'
description: 'Arrange attributes under headings so a product screen and a storefront data sheet read as blocks rather than loose fields'
---

A **group** is a heading that attributes are shown under. Six loose fields become "Technical Info", on the product screen and on your storefront.

Groups are managed from **Attributes** in the main navigation menu.

## What a group holds

A group holds **attributes, not values**. A product's values belong to the attribute itself, which is what makes groups safe to rearrange:

- **Moving an attribute between groups** changes where it is shown and nothing else. No product's value is touched
- **Deleting a group** sets its attributes loose rather than taking them with it. They keep their values and go back to being shown on their own
- **A group reports the attributes assigned to it**, not every attribute you have

## Managing groups

Open the **Groups** panel from the attributes list.

- **Add** a group with a name. The slug is derived from it
- **Rename** and **reorder** them. The order is the order their headings appear in
- **Assign attributes in bulk**, rather than one at a time from each attribute's own form
- **Delete** a group. The confirmation says what will actually happen to the attributes in it

Each attribute's own form has a group picker as well, with **No group** as a real first choice rather than a blank.

## Where groups appear

- **The attributes list** is sectioned by group, in the groups' own order, with everything ungrouped last. A group nothing is in leaves no empty heading, and with no groups at all the list reads exactly as it did before
- **The product screen's attributes panel** renders the same sections, with ungrouped attributes under **Other** when there is something grouped to contrast with
- **Search results** carry the group, so an attribute picked on a product lands under its heading straight away rather than sitting at the bottom until you save and reload

## Changing a group reaches products already using it

A field added to a group **does** turn up on products already stamped with that heading. A group is a set of fields, and a product showing three of its four is not that group any more.

Two cases where it does not:

- A product that only **infers** the heading, where nothing is stamped on it
- A product whose heading the attribute has since **left**

## Data sheets on your storefront

The storefront API sends each attribute's group along with it, so a theme can render a specification table per heading rather than guessing at an order. Crawlers read the same groupings as the product's additional properties.

Private attributes never leave the dashboard, grouped or not. See [Attributes](/attributes/overview).
