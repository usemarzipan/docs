Create an event to sell tickets to a tasting, tour, workshop, class or course. An event can be a one-off or recurring.

To create an event:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Navigate to **Events** in the main menu
3. Click the **Add event** button in the top-right corner

## Creating an event

<Steps titleSize="p">
  <Step title="Name and describe the event">
    Enter the **Name**, **Summary** and **Description** for the event. These are shown to customers.

    The **Description** can take video embed code, so you can paste in a player from Bunny Stream or another video host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add images and video">
    Add images to the event's gallery under **Images**. The gallery can also hold [video](/media/overview#video) from your media library, which plays on the event's page on your storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the event type">
    Select the **Event type**: Tasting, Tour, Workshop, Class, Course or Other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the recurrence">
    Set **Recurring event** to **No - One time event**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**.

    - **Weekly** - use **Repeat on** to pick the weekdays. Leave them all unselected to repeat on the start date's day.
    - **Monthly** - use **Repeat on** to choose **The same date each month**, or a week of the month (**The first** to **The fourth**, or **The last**) with one or more weekdays, for example the first Thursday.

    A recurring event can also run in [separate date ranges](/events/recurring#date-ranges), so a series can pause and pick up again without becoming a second event.

    Recurring events create **occurrences**: individual dated instances that customers book onto. See [Recurring events](/events/recurring).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Use the **All day** toggle, or set the **Times**. Then set the start and end dates.

    Multi-day events are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the location">
    Enter the **Location** as a text address, plus an optional **what3words** address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add email instructions">
    **Email instructions** are added to the ticket email sent to attendees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the tickets and price">
    - **Price** - the ticket price, with optional **Sale price**, **Subscriber price** and **Wholesale price**
    - **Max tickets per order** - the most tickets one customer can buy in a single order. Leave it empty for no limit.
    - **Number of tickets** - the capacity, under **Inventory Management**. Leave it empty for unlimited tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure deposits, check-in and the waitlist">
    Optionally set up **Deposit payments**, **Enable Check-in** and **Enable waitlist**. See below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A monthly event on the same date each month keeps that date. A series that starts on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months and goes back to the 31st when it can, rather than drifting to an earlier date.
</Note>

## Deposits

Turn on **Deposit payments** to let customers pay a deposit to reserve their place rather than the full price upfront:

- **Deposit amount** - the amount taken upfront
- **Days before event to collect balance** - the balance falls due this many days before the event. Tickets bought after that point are charged the full price at the time of purchase.

You can also set a **Ticket sales cutoff** to stop selling tickets a set number of days before the event. Leave it blank for no cutoff.

<Note>
On a recurring event the cut-off is counted from **each date**, so it closes sales for a date a set number of days before that date rather than before the first in the series. A series only reports its sales as closed once every remaining date has passed its own cut-off.
</Note>

<Note>
Outstanding balances can be requested from the event detail screen. See [Details](/events/details#deposits-and-balances).
</Note>

## Check-in and waitlist

- **Enable Check-in** - check attendees in by scanning the QR code on their ticket. See [Details](/events/details#tickets-and-qr-check-in).
- **Enable waitlist** - let customers join a waitlist once tickets sell out. See [Details](/events/details#waitlist).

<Warning>
A waitlist needs a **Number of tickets**. It isn't available for an event with unlimited tickets.
</Warning>

## Duplicating an event

To start a new event from an existing one, open the event's edit page and choose **Duplicate product** from the **Actions** menu. Enter a new **Name**, **Slug** and **SKU**, and choose whether to copy its **Images** and **Reviews**.

The copy is created as a draft and starts with nothing sold, so its ticket count and revenue begin at zero.
