Events let you sell tickets to tastings, tours, workshops, classes and courses. An event can be a one-off or recurring. Recurring events create **occurrences** — individual dated instances that customers book onto.

## Creating an event

When you create an event you can set the following:

- **Name** and **description**
- **Event type** — Tasting, Tour, Workshop, Class, Course or Other
- **Recurrence** — None, Daily, Weekly or Monthly. Weekly lets you pick the weekdays to repeat on, and you can set a recurrence end date
- **All-day** toggle, or a start and end date and time (multi-day events are supported)
- **Location** — a text address, plus an optional **what3words** address
- **Email instructions** — included in the ticket emails sent to attendees
- **Max tickets per order**
- **Price** — the ticket price, with optional sale, subscriber and wholesale prices
- **Capacity** — the quantity of tickets available

### Deposits

You can optionally take a **deposit** rather than the full price upfront. The balance falls due after a set number of days, and you can choose to **auto-charge** it when it does. You can also set a **sales cut-off** to close ticket sales a set number of days before the event.

### Check-in and waitlist

- **Check-in** — enable QR check-in so attendees can be checked in at the door.
- **Waitlist** — enable a waitlist to capture demand once tickets sell out. A waitlist requires a capacity.

## The events list

The events list shows one row per occurrence for recurring events.

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/events/events-list.png" alt="Events list" />
</Frame>

Each row shows:

- A **recurring badge** for recurring events
- The **next date**
- **Tickets sold** out of the total
- **Sold-out** or **waitlist** indicators

You can filter and sort the list:

- Filter by **Status** — Upcoming, Past or Cancelled
- Filter by **Event type**
- Filter by **Recurrence**
- Filter by **Availability**
- Sort by **date** or **tickets sold**

## Recurring events and occurrences

Recurring events give you fine control over each occurrence:

- **Edit one occurrence** — change its date, time, location override or max tickets without affecting the others, so customised occurrence times are preserved
- **Edit all occurrences** — apply changes across the whole series
- **Generate more occurrences** — extend the series with additional dates
- **Cancel a single occurrence** — call off one date while leaving the rest in place

## Managing an event

The event detail screen is organised into tabs.

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/events/event-detail.png" alt="Event detail screen with tabs" />
</Frame>

- **Overview** — tickets sold, revenue and waitlist figures
- **Attendees** — a table of everyone booked on, with a **CSV export**. Columns include customer, tickets, price, deposit/balance, order, checked-in and status. You can filter by status and **bulk check-in** attendees
- **Activity** — the history of actions on the event
- **Waitlist** — position, name, tickets and status; you can add and remove entries

### Attendee actions

From an attendee's panel you can:

- **Check in** the attendee
- **Resend the ticket email**
- **Request balance payment** — for deposit bookings with an outstanding balance
- **Cancel or refund** the booking

The ticket links back to its originating order.

## Tickets and QR check-in

Ticket emails include a **QR code**. At the door, open the QR scanner in the Marzipan app and scan each attendee's code to check them in.

## Deposits and balances

For bookings taken on a deposit, you can request or process the outstanding **balance payment** directly from the event.

## Event Insights

For reporting on attendance, revenue and demand, use **Event Insights**. See [Insights](/insights/overview) for more.
