### Component example
```javascript
<marzipan-product
  product="product-slug"
  view="mini"
  show-cart-quantity="true"
  show-collection-name="true"
  show-more-link="false"
  show-more-text="More"
  redirect-to-cart="false"
  show-subscriber-pricing="false"
></marzipan-product>
```

### Views

The product component supports two different view modes:

#### Mini View
The mini view displays the product in a compact format suitable for product grids or collections. It includes the product image, name, price, and add to cart functionality.

#### Full View  
The full view displays the product in an expanded format suitable for dedicated product pages. It includes:
- Image gallery with thumbnails
- Detailed product information
- Product options and variants
- Full description
- Product attributes
- Awards and reviews
- Related products

### Features

#### Subscription Products
The product component automatically detects and handles subscription products, including pick-and-mix subscriptions. For subscription products, it integrates with the subscription configuration component to provide frequency selection and item management.

#### Quick Attributes
When enabled in your [web component settings](/web-components/customisation#quick-attributes), key product attributes (e.g. Size, Colour, Material) are displayed directly under the product name. You choose which attributes to show and their display order from **Settings > Branding & Web Components > Product**.

#### Purchase Options

Where a product has upsells, the page offers them either as a **dropdown** or as **priced boxes** showing every option's price at once. Set which under [Upsell Options](/web-components/customisation#product-behaviour), and add an optional heading above them with the **Upsell Picker Heading** label. The product's own row is named with the **Base Option Label**, and where you show subscriber pricing, each row uses your **Subscriber Price Label**.

#### One-off or Subscribe

A product that a Fixed [package](/subscriptions/packages) sends can be bought once or subscribed to from its own page. Subscribing puts the package in the cart instead of the product, at the quantity chosen and on the frequency picked.

- **Without upsells**, the page asks **How would you like it?** with a row for **One-off purchase** and one for **Subscribe**. Where more than one package sends the product, each row is named after its package.
- **With upsells**, the subscribe rows join the upsell list, straight after the product's own row, rather than a second chooser beneath it. The product's own row is the one-off, and choosing to subscribe leaves any upsell, since the subscription is to this product.

Each subscribe row shows the package's price with the one-off price struck through beside it, and a note saying how often it's sent, the fewest that can be subscribed to, what it saves and whether delivery is free, for example "Monthly. Minimum 3. Save 10%". Where the package is sold at more than one frequency, the note leaves out how often, and choosing the row opens the frequency inside it. With upsells shown as a dropdown, the frequency is asked below instead.

The page opens on the one-off purchase unless you set **Purchase Option Selected First** to **Subscribe** on the [Product tab](/web-components/customisation#product-behaviour). Every piece of wording here is on the [Labels tab](/web-components/customisation#labels).

#### Billing Frequency

A subscription package sold at more than one billing frequency lets the shopper choose, as **boxes** or a **dropdown**. The frequency they pick is what the subscription is created on.

The heading above the choice is set with the **Frequency Picker Heading** label.

#### Text Options

A product with a [short or long text option](/products/bundles-and-options#options), such as an engraving or a gift message, shows a text box with a character count. The add to cart button stays disabled until any required text is filled in. A mini view card has nowhere to type, so it links to the product page with the **See Options Button** wording instead of adding to the cart.

#### Quantity Limits

Where a product has a per-customer limit, its quantity stepper stops at the limit and says **Max** followed by the number **per customer**.

#### Gift Vouchers

A gift voucher at a fixed price adds straight to the cart. The amount chooser only opens when there's an amount to pick or one to type.

#### Accordion Sections (Full View)
The full view product page uses configurable accordion sections. You can control which sections appear, their order, and whether they default to open or collapsed. Available sections include description, attributes, reviews, awards, press, related products, and custom text blocks. Configure these in your [web component settings](/web-components/customisation#accordion-sections).

#### Pricing Display
The component displays pricing based on:
- Sale prices vs regular prices
- Subscriber pricing for logged-in subscribers
- Bundle pricing with custom text
- Price badges for sales and subscriber discounts
- Multi-currency pricing based on the customer's market

#### Image Gallery (Full View)
The full view includes a gallery with:
- The main image, with arrows and dots to move through the gallery on a phone
- A thumbnail strip down the side or along the bottom. It's held to the size of the main image and scrolls when there are more than fit, with an arrow at each end when there's more that way, keeping the current image's thumbnail in view
- Videos, which show as their poster with a play badge in the strip and play in the gallery's player. A video only plays when the shopper chooses it, never because it's first in the gallery. Set the player's controls and colour on the [Video tab](/web-components/customisation#video), and see [adding videos](/products/create) to a product
- A full-screen overlay, if **Enable image overlay** is on
- Fallback placeholder when no images are available

#### SEO Features (Full View)
The full view automatically injects:
- Meta tags for social sharing
- Structured data (JSON-LD) for search engines
- A page title with the product's name, keeping your site's name at the end
- Product availability and pricing information

The product component uses the [add to cart](./add-to-cart) component to handle cart functionality. If you have set styles for the add to cart component, they will be applied to the buttons within this component.
