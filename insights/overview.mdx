**Insights** is Marzipan's reporting area. Each report gives you a focused view of a different part of your business, from revenue and customers through to subscriptions, rewards and web traffic.

Every report shares the same core controls:

- **Date range** — set the period the report covers.
- **Compared to** — compare the current period against a previous one to see how things are trending.
- **Market filter** — narrow the report to a single market if you sell across more than one.

Test-mode orders are excluded from Insights, so every report reflects real trading only.

Access to each report is controlled by your [Roles](/settings/overview) permissions, so you can decide which team members see which reports.

## Revenue

Your top-line sales performance over the selected period.

- Total sales
- Average order value
- Average daily revenue
- Conversion rate

You can add extra columns to break the numbers down further, including orders, units sold, new and returning customers, discounts, refunds, shipping, tax, subscriptions, events and products.

## Channels

See how your sales channels compare.

- Revenue, orders and average order value by sales channel
- Your top channel

## Geographic

- Sales broken down by location

## Cart recovery

Understand how well you're recovering abandoned carts.

- Conversion rate
- Recovered vs lost revenue
- Average recovery time
- An abandonment funnel
- Recovery by source

## Customers

A view of your customer base and how well you retain it.

- Total, active and new customers
- Repeat-purchase and retention rates
- Average lifetime value

It also includes an **RFM analysis** with customer segments, a customer lifetime value trend, and your top customers.

## Rewards

Track how your loyalty programme is performing.

- Members
- Points earned, redeemed and outstanding
- Redemption rate
- Member vs non-member average order value, and AOV lift
- Tier distribution
- Tier upgrades and downgrades

## Products

- Revenue, units sold and average price
- Drill down into any individual product

## Subscriptions

Key metrics for your recurring revenue.

- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) and annual recurring revenue (ARR)
- Churn and retention rates
- Average subscription value and duration

Subscriptions also includes sub-reports for **Status** (distribution and transitions between statuses), **Billing** (failed payments), and **Additional items**.

## Orders

- Total orders
- Orders per day
- Average items per order
- Fulfilment rate

## Attribution

See where your orders come from, fed by the checkout "how did you hear about us?" question and UTM tracking.

- Tracked visits
- Attributed orders and revenue
- Attribution rate
- **UTM source, medium and campaign** breakdowns

## Events

- Event performance metrics

## Web analytics

For Marzipan-hosted sites, Web analytics shows how visitors are using your website.

- Live **visitors online now**
- Top pages, with pageviews, uniques and average duration
- Top referrers and sources
- Breakdowns by page, referrer, country and device

Until analytics is set up for your site, this report shows a "not configured" state.
