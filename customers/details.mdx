Opening a customer from the [customers list](/customers/overview) takes you to their profile. This is the full picture of one customer: who they are, what they have bought, what you have sent them, and where they stand in your loyalty programme.

</Frame>

The profile is organised into tabs.

### Overview

The Overview tab gives you an at-a-glance picture of the customer:

- **Highlight cards** - total and average spend, how long they have been a customer for, and their number of orders, subscriptions and events
- **Activity feed** - a running history of the customer, including any notes you add. It updates automatically
- **Latest orders and subscriptions** - their most recent records

### Activity

The full activity timeline for the customer, with the option to add a note. Older entries load as you scroll down.

### Orders, Subscriptions, Events and Gift Vouchers

Each of these tabs lists the customer's records of that type, so you can see everything they have ordered, subscribed to or attended, and every [gift voucher](/gift-vouchers/overview) sent to them or spent by them.

### Emails

A record of every email sent to the customer, showing its subject, which notification it was, when it was sent and last updated, and its delivery status. That includes the emails sent on their behalf from somewhere other than their account, such as back-in-stock alerts and event waitlist emails, which are matched to the customer by email address when they signed up as a guest.

Each email has a delivery status:

- `Queued`
- `Sent`
- `Delivered`
- `Bounced`
- `Spam`
- `Failed`

Open any email to see its detail view, with each step of its delivery. An email that has been sent again is marked **Resend**, and the original it replaced **Resent**.

### Rewards

When rewards are enabled, this tab shows the customer's points and tier history. You can **Adjust** their points balance or set their tier directly from here. See [Rewards](/rewards/overview).

## Panels

Alongside the tabs, the profile shows a set of panels you can manage.

### Details

Name, email (with a verified/email-status indicator), phone, company, date of birth, registered date, market, source and tags.

### Addresses

The customer's saved addresses, along with any delivery instructions. You can add, edit and remove addresses, and set which one is the default.

### Payment Methods

The customer's saved cards, with their expiry dates and which is the default. Click **Manage** to add or remove cards.

### Mailing Lists

Toggles for each of your mailing lists, so you can keep the customer's marketing consent up to date.

### Rewards

When rewards are enabled, a block showing the customer's tier, points and progress towards the next tier. See [Rewards](/rewards/overview).

### Tags

Tags are free-form labels you can add to a customer to help you group and filter them. When rewards are enabled, the customer's reward tier also appears as a tag.

## Actions

The **Actions** menu at the top of the profile holds:

- **Manually verify email** - mark the customer's email address as verified. Only shown while it isn't
- **Send password reset email** - email the customer a link to set a new password for their account (see below)
- **Archive customer** / **Unarchive customer** - archive a customer you no longer deal with, or restore them. Archiving can also unsubscribe them from all mailing lists

From the panels on the profile you can also:

- **Edit details** - update their name, contact details and other information
- **Manage addresses and payment methods**
- **Manage marketing consent**
- **Manage tags**
- **Adjust points or set tier** - when rewards are enabled, from the **Rewards** tab

### Sending a password reset

When a customer is locked out of their account, choose **Send password reset email** from the **Actions** menu and click **Send email** to confirm. They're emailed a link to your store's account page where they can choose a new password.

- The link lasts for **an hour**
- Their current password keeps working until they use the link
- The send is recorded in the customer's activity, as sent by you rather than requested by the customer
- Only one reset can be sent a minute. If you try again sooner, you're told so rather than another email going out

Sending a reset needs the **Customers - Edit** permission.

<Note>
The customer's **type** (`Direct` or `Wholesale`) is set when the customer is [created](/customers/create) and cannot be changed from the profile.
</Note>
