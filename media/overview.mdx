The Media library is your central store of images and videos. Media you add here is reused across products, events, subscription packages, bottle labels, share images, CMS content, and the rich-text editor.

To open your media library:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Click on **Media** in the main navigation menu

## Library

</Frame>

The library shows your media in a grid. Each image displays its **thumbnail**, **filename**, **alt text**, and **date**, and each video shows its title. Use the **Show** menu to see **All media**, **Images** or **Videos**, and sort the grid by date.

## Uploading

There are two ways to add media:

- Click **Upload**, choose a file from your device, and add its alt text, or its title for a video. Click **Done** to save and close.
- **Drag and drop** files straight onto the page. A **Describe Your Uploads** step then lets you add alt text to each image and a title to each video before they're saved.

Images can be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10MB each. Videos can be MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 2GB each.

## Video

Video can be added to product, event and subscription package galleries alongside images, and plays on that page of your storefront. A ready video can also be placed in rich-text content with the editor's media picker.

After a video uploads it shows **Processing video** while it's prepared for streaming. This can take up to 15 minutes. It goes live by itself, so you can leave the page. If a video can't be processed, its tile says so.

- A video's **title** works as its alt text. Screen readers announce it as the name of the video player.
- A video is never a product's main image, and isn't used anywhere that can only show a still, such as Google Shopping, e-labels and event cards.
- Fields that need a picture, such as CMS images and share images, only offer images.

<Tip>
Set the video player's controls and colour on the **Video** tab under **Settings > Branding & Web Components**.
</Tip>

## Managing media

- Click an image to edit its **alt text**, or a video to edit its **title**
- **Delete** media you no longer need. If it's used elsewhere, Marzipan tells you how many other items use it, since deleting removes it from all of them.
- Media that's in use can be **unlinked** from a record instead of deleted

### Replacing a single image

Fields that hold one image, such as your logo or default share image, have a **Replace** button on the image. It opens the media library so you can pick a different image without deleting the current one first.

<Tip>
Good alt text describes what's in the image. It improves accessibility for people using screen readers and helps your SEO.
</Tip>
