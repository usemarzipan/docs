The Media library is your central store of images. Images you add here are reused across products, bottle labels, share images, CMS content, and the rich-text editor.

To open your media library:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Click on **Media** in the main navigation menu

## Library

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/media/media-library.png" alt="Media library" />
</Frame>

The library shows your images in a grid. Each image displays its **thumbnail**, **filename**, **alt text**, and **date**. You can sort the grid by date.

## Uploading images

There are two ways to add images:

- Click **Upload** to choose files from your device and add alt text
- **Drag and drop** images straight onto the page

Images can be PNG, JPG, or GIF, up to 10 MB each. After a drop, a bulk alt-text step lets you caption each image before it's saved.

## Managing images

- Click an image to edit its **alt text**
- **Delete** an image you no longer need
- An image that's in use can be **unlinked** from a record

<Tip>
Good alt text describes what's in the image. It improves accessibility for people using screen readers and helps your SEO.
</Tip>
