Collections are manual groupings of products. Use them to organise your catalogue and to power the product listings on your storefront.

<Note>
Collections are always manual — you choose exactly which products they contain. There are no automatic or rule-based collections.
</Note>

To view your collections:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Click on **Collections** in the main navigation menu

## Collections list

<Frame>
<img src="https://marzipan-cloud.b-cdn.net/0/docs/collections/collections-list.png" alt="Collections list" />
</Frame>

The list shows each collection's **name**, **slug**, **status**, **number of products**, and when it was **last updated**. Click **Add collection** to create a new one.

## Creating and editing a collection

- **Name** — the collection name
- **Description** — a description of the collection
- **Published** — when on, the collection is live on your storefront
- **Products** — add the products in the collection, and **drag to reorder** them. The order controls how products display on your storefront. You can also add subscription packages and events.
- **Attributes** — set collection-level [attribute](/attributes/overview) values
