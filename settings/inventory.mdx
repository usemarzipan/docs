Inventory settings let you track stock across one or more locations, record every change, and warn customers and your team when stock runs low.

Navigate to **Settings > Inventory** to manage your inventory.

## Location-based inventory

Location-based inventory is **off by default**. To start tracking stock per location, **initialise** it once. This copies your current stock to your default location, after which stock is managed on each product.

Until you initialise it, a banner on this page prompts you to do so, with a default-location picker if you haven't set one yet, and the inventory page shows a callout with its inputs disabled.

<Warning>
Initialising location-based inventory **can't be undone**. Make sure your current stock figures are correct before you start.
</Warning>

### Inventory locations

Under **Inventory Locations**, add the locations you hold stock in, each with a **Name** and **Address**, and set one as your **Default Location**.

Every [market](/settings/markets) names the location its stock comes from, and the storefront shows each market the stock at its own location. A location a market uses, including an archived market, can't be deleted until the market is moved to another one.

### Inventory rules

Once you have more than one location, add **Inventory Rules** to choose which location fulfils an order based on conditions such as the shipping country or billing country, or whether the order is delivered or collected.

An order's location is chosen in this order, using the first that applies:

1. The shipping method's **Fulfil from** location, if it names one (see [Shipping](/settings/shipping#fulfil-from))
2. An inventory rule whose conditions the order meets
3. The order's market's location
4. Your default location

## Stock movements

Every change to stock is recorded as a movement with a reason, so you always have a full history. Reasons include:

- **Received**
- **Write off**
- **Stock take**
- **Transfer** (in or out)
- **Return**
- **Order**
- **Wholesale**
- **Initial stock**
- **Derived**
- **Other**

You can add optional notes to any movement.

A **Wholesale** adjustment can name the trade customer it was made for. When you name one, the adjustment also appears on that customer's activity feed. The picker only offers wholesale accounts, and naming a customer is optional.

**Derived** movements are recorded automatically for bundles taking their stock from [constituent items](/products/inventory#stock-source).

Cancelling an order returns its stock automatically, to the location it originally came off, recorded as a **Return**.

## Adjusting stock

Use **Adjust stock** on the inventory page to correct several products in one go, which is what a wholesale order with multiple lines needs. Add products by search, give each line its own quantity up or down, then set one location, reason, note and optional trade customer across the whole batch. The current stock at the chosen location is shown against each line as you go.

The batch is applied together, so a single bad line can't leave an adjustment half applied.

You can also **import a CSV** with one row per SKU per location.

## Exporting inventory

Click **Export** on the inventory page to download the current filtered view as a CSV, with one column per inventory location plus a total. Filter the list first to export only the products you need.

<Tip>
The inventory page also links straight to the [stock valuation report](/insights/sales#stock-valuation), so you can go from the figures to what they're worth.
</Tip>

## Low-stock badges

Show customers when stock is running low with a storefront badge. These are global defaults that each product can override.

- Turn on **Enable Low Stock Badges** and set a **Low Stock Threshold**
- Set the **Badge Text**, which supports a `{count}` placeholder for the remaining quantity
- Override the badge [per product](/products/inventory#low-stock-badge) where needed

## Low-stock alerts

Email your team when a product's stock falls to a set level.

- Turn on **Enable Low Stock Alerts** and set an **Alert Threshold**. You're notified when stock falls to or below it.
- Choose an **Alert Mode**:
  - **Real-time** - Sent immediately, at most one per product every 24 hours
  - **Daily digest** - A single email at 8am listing every product below the threshold

With location-based inventory, alerts look at each location on its own. A product is alerted when any of its locations runs low, even if another location holds plenty, and only once per location until that location is restocked above the threshold. The email lists the product once, with the stock at every location.

<Note>
Only team members who have opted into the low-stock notification receive these alerts. Manage this under **Settings > Team**.
</Note>

## London City Bond

If you use the [London City Bond](/integrations/london-city-bond) integration, stock syncs automatically into a dedicated inventory location. This requires location-based inventory to be initialised first.
