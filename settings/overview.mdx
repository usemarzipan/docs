The Settings section is where you configure your store's core options, payment providers, integrations, and appearance. Access it from the main navigation sidebar.

## General

Your store's basic information and preferences.

- **Store name** — Your business name, displayed in emails and invoices
- **Contact email** — Where customer enquiries and contact form submissions are sent
- **Country** — Your store's primary country (used for default address formatting and tax)
- **Currency** — Your base currency for pricing and reporting
- **Timezone** — Used for order timestamps, scheduled tasks, and reporting

## Payments

Configure your payment gateways. Marzipan supports:

- **Stripe** — Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, and more. Includes Stripe Connect for marketplace payments.
- **PayPal** — PayPal checkout for customers who prefer it.
- **PayFast** — Credit cards, instant EFT, and local South African payment methods.

Each gateway can store both live and test credentials, which are toggled automatically by [test mode](/settings/test-mode).

## Web Components

Customise the appearance and behaviour of your customer-facing web components without writing code. See the [Web Components Customisation guide](/web-components/customisation) for full details.

## Integrations

Connect your store to third-party services:

- **Mailchimp** — Sync customers and subscribers to your Mailchimp audience with configurable merge field mapping
- **London City Bond** — Automatic order fulfilment through LCB's bonded warehouse
- **ScrubBill** — Third-party logistics and fulfilment
- **Fathom Analytics** — View website analytics directly in your Marzipan dashboard

## Email

Manage your email sending domains and delivery settings.

- **Sending domains** — Register and verify custom sending domains with DNS records
- **Delivery status** — Monitor email delivery with bounce and suppression tracking

## API

Generate API tokens for integrating with external systems or powering your web components. Each token has configurable permission scopes.

## Developer Settings

Tools for development and testing.

- **Test Mode** — A global toggle that switches all payment gateways to their test/sandbox credentials. Use this to test the full purchase flow without processing real payments.
