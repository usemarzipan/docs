title: 'Payments'
description: 'Process and manage payments for orders'

Marzipan tracks the payment status of every order and lets you take payment, record offline payments, and issue refunds from the order details page.

### Payment status

Each order has a payment status:

- `pending` - payment has not yet been taken
- `paid` - payment has been received in full
- `failed` - a payment attempt was unsuccessful
- `refunded` - the order has been refunded (fully or partially)
- `free` - a zero-value order that has been completed

### Process payment

On an unpaid order, click **Process payment** to take payment. You can either:

- **Charge a saved card** - charge a payment method the customer has on file
- **Record a manual payment** - log a payment taken offline, for example cash, bank transfer, phone, or other

Use a manual payment when the customer has already paid you outside of Marzipan and you just need to record it against the order.

Whichever you choose, the order then carries on as a paid order: event tickets are issued and any [flows](/flows/overview) on the `order.paid` trigger run, such as sending the order to London City Bond. An order on `hold` waits until it's released.

When a payment arrives it's recorded in the order's activity, along with how it was made: through checkout, from the customer's own account, or with a payment link.

### Send payment link

Rather than taking the card yourself, you can send the customer a link to pay the order themselves. Available on unpaid orders that settle through Stripe. See [Payment links](/orders/payment-links).

### Mark as free

For zero-value orders, use **Mark as free** to complete the order without taking payment.

<Warning>
Marking an order as free triggers the same automations as a paid order, so a warning is shown before you confirm.
</Warning>

### Refunds

Choose **Refund order** from the order's **Actions** menu to refund it in full or in part. Pick the payment to refund, add a reason, and optionally tick **Cancel order** to cancel the order once the refund has gone through. The refund is shown against the original payment as a nested row on the order.

Payments and refunds are always shown in the order's own currency, so a euro order reads in euros throughout, whatever your default currency.

<Note>
PayFast has no refund API, so a refund against a PayFast payment doesn't move any money. Refund the customer in your PayFast dashboard, then record it in Marzipan to keep the order and your reports right.
</Note>

### Redeem points

When rewards are enabled, you can **Redeem points** at the order level to apply the customer's rewards points to the order total.

### Tap to Pay and card-present payments

Card payments taken in person appear on the order with a Tap to Pay indicator, along with the card brand and the last 4 digits of the card. These are taken through the Marzipan mobile app. See [Point of Sale](/orders/pos).
