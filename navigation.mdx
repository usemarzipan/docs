The main navigation menu is shown in the sidebar on every page. On a smaller screen, click the menu button to open it. You only see the areas your [role](/settings/roles-and-team) gives you access to.

The menu is grouped into sections:

- **Home**, **Insights** and **Tasks** at the top
- **Sales** - **Orders**, **Abandoned Carts**, **Discounts** and **Gift Vouchers**
- **Products** - **Products**, **Collections**, **Attributes** and **Inventory**
- **Subscriptions** - **Packages**, **Subscriptions** and **Shipments**
- **Events**
- **Customers** - **Customers**, **Rewards**, **Forms** and **Messages**
- **Content** - **Pages**, **Posts**, **Collections** and **Media**
- **Automation** - **Flows**, for stores with Flows switched on

Some items show a count: **Orders** shows how many orders are processing, and **Subscriptions**, **Tasks** and **Messages** show what's waiting for you there.

On a wide screen you can collapse the sidebar to icons with the **Collapse menu** button at the top, and expand it again the same way.

### Search / Command Palette

You can access the search/command palette by clicking on the search icon at the top of the sidebar (or top centre on mobile).

### Account Details and settings

Less frequently used items are in the menu under your name at the bottom of the sidebar: **Account** for your own details, **Settings** for your store's [settings](/settings/overview), **Support** to email us, and **Log out**.
