Marzipan includes a CMS for managing your site's content. You can build pages out of blocks, publish posts, and group content into collections, all from your dashboard, and read every bit of it back over the API.

To open the CMS:

1. Log in to your Marzipan account
2. Find the **Content** section in the main navigation menu
3. Click **Pages**, **Posts**, **Collections** or **Media** to work on that part of your site

## How content is structured

The CMS is built from three pieces that work together.

| Area | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [**Pages**](/cms/pages) | Content pages built as an ordered list of blocks |
| [**Posts**](/cms/posts) | Blog-style articles with an excerpt, content, author and images |
| [**Collections**](/cms/collections) | Ordered groupings of pages and posts, used to build navigation and listings |

Pages and posts hold the content. Collections organise that content into menus and listings. What a block looks like belongs to your storefront, which declares the block types it can render.

<Note>
The **Website** area has gone. Templates and shared files belonged to pages Marzipan rendered itself, and a storefront now owns its own layout and assets. Your store's address and the domains pointed at it are under [Settings > Website](/settings/website).
</Note>

## Headless CMS API

All of your content is also available over the **headless CMS API**, so you can pull pages, posts and collections into other front ends:

- `/v1/cms/pages`
- `/v1/cms/posts`
- `/v1/cms/collections`
- `/v1/cms/schema`

See the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction) to get started.
