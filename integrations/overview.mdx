Integrations connect your store to third-party services for fulfilment, couriers, marketing, finance, and labelling.

Navigate to **Settings > Integrations**, which shows two lists: **Installed** integrations and **Available** integrations. To install one, click it. Each integration shows whether it's `active` or `inactive`, along with a test-mode badge that follows the global [test mode](/settings/test-mode) toggle.

## Managing an integration

Each integration has its own settings page once installed, where you configure its options. Open it from the **Installed** list at any time.

## Moving from WooCommerce

If you're moving to Marzipan from WooCommerce, we can bring your store's history across for you: customers and account holders, orders with their payments, refunds and discount codes, and coupons as discounts. Simple products, and variable products priced the same across their variations, can come too, with their images, and categories become collections.

Records are matched before anything is created, so an import can be run again to pick up anything new without making duplicates. Coupon rules Marzipan can't express are reported rather than dropped quietly. [Get in touch](mailto:hello@marzipan.co) to arrange an import.

## Don't see the one you need?

We add integrations as merchants ask for them, and more are on the way. If the service you work with isn't listed, [get in touch](mailto:hello@marzipan.co) and tell us what you need it to do. Building one is often a good deal quicker than you'd expect.

It's worth asking even where a service already has a partner integration elsewhere. Knowing which parts of it you actually use is what makes a good one.
